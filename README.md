# samo3aliko

Games we're making together as a family.

## 🚕 Samo3aliko Taxi

A top-down taxi game. Pick people up, drive them where they want to go, earn fares and tips, and spend your money in the garage.

**To play:** open `taxi-game/index.html` in any web browser (double-click it). Nothing to install.

### Controls

| Keyboard | Touch screen | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ↑ or W | ⏩ | Gas |
| ↓ or S | ⏪ | Brake, then reverse |
| ← → or A D | ◀ ▶ | Steer |
| Space | | Handbrake drift |
| Shift | 🔥 | Turbo (buy it in the garage first) |
| H | 📯 | Honk |
| G | 🔧 | Garage |

### How it works

- Yellow circles are people waiting. Stop next to one to pick them up.
- Follow the green arrow to the 🏁 and stop there to drop them off.
- Get there before the timer runs out and don't crash, and they'll tip you more.
- Your money and upgrades are saved in the browser.

### Make it your own

Open `taxi-game/index.html` in a text editor and look for **KIDS' CORNER** at the top of the code. You can change:

- `CITY`: the map. Every emoji is a city block. Swap them, or add rows and columns.
- `PLACES`: the names and roof colours of places.
- `PEOPLE`: passengers, what they say, and how much they pay.
- `CAR`: how fast the car goes, how it turns, and how slippery it is.
- `UPGRADES`, `VEHICLES`, `PAINTS`: what the garage sells and for how much.
- `GAME_NAME` and `MADE_BY`: put your own names in!

Save the file and refresh the browser to see your changes.
