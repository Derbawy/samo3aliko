How to play
  1. Read the brief: a theme, a mystery ingredient you must use, and three judges.
  2. Tap ingredients to plate them raw or cook them in a pan or pot.
  3. Pull food off the heat while the needle is in the green. Wait too long and it burns.
  4. Serve before the clock hits zero. Food still on the stove when time runs out is lost.
  5. The judges score your dish against your rival's, out of 30.
